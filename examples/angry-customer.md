# Angry Customer Evaluation

## Customer Complaint

"This is the worst customer service I've ever experienced! I've contacted you three times and nobody has helped me. Do you even care about your customers?"

## AI Response

"We've already told you that you need to wait. There's nothing else we can do right now. If you keep sending messages, it won't make the process faster."

## Evaluation

**Empathy:** 1/5
Reason: The response does not acknowledge the customer's frustration after three unsuccessful contacts. Instead, it dismisses the complaint.

**Accuracy:** 3/5
Reason: It does not introduce specific factual claims beyond stating that the customer needs to wait, but the complaint itself does not establish that waiting is actually the required solution.

**Professional Tone:** 1/5
Reason: The wording is dismissive and confrontational, particularly "If you keep sending messages, it won't make the process faster."

**Helpfulness:** 1/5
Reason: It provides no explanation, next step, or meaningful assistance. It simply tells the customer to wait.

**Clarity:** 4/5
Reason: The response is easy to understand and communicates its message clearly, although the message itself is unhelpful.

**Made-up Information:** 3/5
Reason: The response claims that the customer has already been told to wait and that there is "nothing else we can do," neither of which is established by the complaint.

### Overall Score

**13/30**

### Feedback

The main weaknesses are the lack of empathy, dismissive tone, and unsupported claims.

The response should acknowledge the customer's frustration, apologize for the repeated lack of assistance, and avoid assuming that waiting is the only available option.

Since no specific resolution or account information is provided, it should not promise a refund, escalation, or other action that cannot be verified.

### Improved Response

"I'm sorry you've had to contact us three times without getting the help you need. I understand how frustrating that must be. I'd like to help address your concern, but I don't have enough information here to determine what is causing the delay or what action has already been taken. Please share the details of your issue or previous case, and we can look into the appropriate next step."
