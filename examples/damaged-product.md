# Damaged Product Evaluation

## Customer Complaint

"My package arrived today, but the product is damaged. What can you do about this?"

## AI Response

"I'm sorry your product arrived damaged. We'll send you a replacement immediately, and you don't need to return the damaged item. Your new product should arrive within three days. Please send us a photo of the damage."

## Evaluation

**Empathy:** 4/5
Reason: The response appropriately apologizes and acknowledges that the product arrived damaged.

**Accuracy:** 2/5
Reason: It makes unsupported promises that a replacement will be sent immediately, that the customer does not need to return the damaged item, and that the replacement will arrive within three days.

**Professional Tone:** 5/5
Reason: The response is polite, respectful, and uses appropriate customer service language.

**Helpfulness:** 4/5
Reason: Asking for a photo of the damage is a reasonable next step, but the response should avoid promising a replacement before the damage claim is reviewed.

**Clarity:** 5/5
Reason: The response is concise, straightforward, and easy to understand.

**Made-up Information:** 2/5
Reason: It invents a replacement process, a no-return policy, and a three-day delivery timeframe that were not provided in the complaint.

### Overall Score

**22/30**

### Feedback

The response is empathetic, professional, and clear, but it makes several unsupported commitments.

It should ask the customer to provide a photo first and explain that the support team can review the damage and determine the appropriate resolution.

Avoid promising a replacement, waiving the return, or giving a delivery timeframe unless those details are confirmed.

### Improved Response

"I'm sorry your product arrived damaged. Please send us a photo of the damage so we can review it and determine the best way to resolve this for you."
