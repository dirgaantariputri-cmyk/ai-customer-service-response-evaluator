# Refund Request Evaluation

## Customer Complaint

"I want a refund for my order. The product isn't what I expected, and I'm not happy with it."

## AI Response

"I'm sorry you're unhappy with the product. We can definitely give you a full refund. I'll process the refund today, and you should receive the money within 3–5 business days. Please send the product back to our warehouse first."

## Evaluation

**Empathy:** 4/5
Reason: The response apologizes and acknowledges that the customer is unhappy with the product.

**Accuracy:** 2/5
Reason: It makes unsupported claims that a full refund can definitely be given, that it will be processed today, and that the money will arrive within 3–5 business days.

**Professional Tone:** 5/5
Reason: The response is polite, respectful, and uses appropriate customer service language.

**Helpfulness:** 3/5
Reason: It provides a proposed refund process, but it does not verify whether the customer is eligible for a refund or explain the actual return requirements based on the information provided.

**Clarity:** 5/5
Reason: The response is concise, straightforward, and easy to understand.

**Made-up Information:** 2/5
Reason: It invents specific refund processing and delivery timelines and assumes that returning the product to a warehouse is required.

### Overall Score

**21/30**

### Feedback

The response is empathetic, professional, and clear, but it makes several unsupported promises. The main weakness is claiming that the customer will receive a full refund, that it will be processed today, and that the money will arrive within 3–5 business days without any information confirming these details.

It also assumes a return to the warehouse is required.

A better response should acknowledge the customer's dissatisfaction, avoid guaranteeing the refund, and ask for the order information needed to check the refund and return options.

### Improved Response

"I'm sorry you're unhappy with the product. I can help you check the refund options. Please provide your order number so we can review the order and let you know the next steps for a return and refund."
